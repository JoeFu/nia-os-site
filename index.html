N.I.A

A self-hosted family assistant. It runs on the household's own computer (N.I.A OS), not as a public service.

Google Calendar

“nia-os” is the Google sign-in N.I.A uses so that each person in the household can connect their own Google Calendar. Once connected, N.I.A can:

  • list that person's calendars and show their upcoming events when they ask;
  • create, change or delete an event, and only after the person confirms each change.

Nobody can see or change anyone else's calendar through N.I.A. Each person can disconnect at any time in N.I.A, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Privacy policy

简介

N.I.A 是一个自己部署的家庭助手，运行在家里自己的电脑上（N.I.A OS），不是对外开放的服务。

「nia-os」是 N.I.A 用来连接 Google 日历的授权。家里每个人连接的都是自己的 Google 日历；连接后，N.I.A 能在你问的时候列出你的日历和日程，并在你逐条确认后新建、修改或删除日程。谁也不能通过 N.I.A 看或改别人的日历。随时可以在 N.I.A 里断开，或者到 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销。
